📦 Delivery Performance & Fleet Efficiency Dashboard
Power BI Assignment – 3 | Logistics & Fleet Analytics

📋 Project Description

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of delivery operations and fleet efficiency across major Indian cities. It covers trip performance, vehicle fuel efficiency, driver productivity, cost analysis, and actionable insights — all built on a dark-themed, interactive 4-page report.

✅ Key Findings

Good Insights

	•	Mumbai achieved 100% on-time delivery — top performing city
	•	Kolkata maintained 86% on-time rate — second best
	•	Mini-Truck is the most fuel efficient at 12.50 km/liter
	•	Drivers D03 and D05 achieved 100% on-time delivery
	•	Fleet covered 53,607 KM showing strong utilization

Areas to Improve

	•	Overall on-time delivery is only 60% — 40% trips are late
	•	Bangalore has the worst rate at just 17%
	•	Driver D07 has only 28.57% on-time rate — needs retraining
	•	Mumbai → Bangalore is the most expensive route at ~₹40K
	•	Truck has the lowest fuel efficiency at 10.98 km/liter

🎯 Recommendations

	1.	Fix Bangalore & Chennai delivery routes immediately
	2.	Retrain or reassign drivers D07, D02, and D06
	3.	Service or replace Trucks due to low fuel efficiency
	4.	Optimize Mumbai → Bangalore route to reduce fuel costs
	5.	Set 80% on-time delivery as the minimum KPI target
	6.	Investigate ₹76K maintenance cost for cost reduction
